What Pip is

Pip is a baby with a smooth, round skull face, big soft eye sockets glowing with warm amber light, a tiny crooked smile, and a starry nightcap with a pink bow. Cute, never scary — the skull is just Pip's face, like a cozy little moon.

Pip is curious, sleepy, a little clumsy, and giggles at everything. Pip's one fear? Silence. That's why the nursery always hums a lullaby. By night, Pip works as the Junior Dream Engineer of the Midnight Nursery, designing the dreams, lullabies, and nightlights that tuck the neighborhood's little ghosts and beasties into bed.

  • 💀 face: skull, but make it cozy
  • 👀 eyes: glowing amber pinpricks
  • 🎀 hat: starry nightcap + pink bow
  • 😄 laugh: giggles at everything
  • 🔇 fear: silence (hates it)

What Pip makes

Four inventions from the nursery workshop. They're all playable — go ahead, poke them.

1 · The Lullaby Lab

Pip's music box composes a brand-new soothing tune every night. Press play to hear tonight's lullaby, synthesized live in your browser — no recordings, just Web Audio magic. Side effects may include drowsiness.

Tip: the speaker button (bottom-right) mutes everything.

2 · Nightlight Collection

A gallery of glowing lamps Pip designed for monsters who are scared of the dark. Flip the nursery to night mode and watch everything change moods — the crescent moon lantern is Pip's proudest build.

3 · Dream Delivery Routes

Every night Pip flies rooftop to rooftop delivering dreams. This is tonight's route over Willow Lane — watch the little dream dot make its rounds while Pip naps between stops.

4 · Pip's Growth Chart

Every good nursery wall has pencil marks tracking how big the baby got. Pip's wall tracks something better: skills, measured in centimeters of pure capability.

5 · Rattle & Block Toys

Pip invented dream blocks that only stack when you're brave. Press start, then tap the tower to drop each swinging block — line it up, or the wobbly bit tumbles off. How high can you go before bedtime?

Pip's story

How Pip got here, in three small scenes. Scroll slowly.

🌫️

Scene 1 · The arrival

Pip arrived at the Midnight Nursery on a foggy Tuesday, wrapped in a blanket, holding an empty dream jar. Nobody knows where Pip came from. Pip doesn't remember either — but Pip remembers the lullaby.

🎓

Scene 2 · The hiring

The nursery elders tasted Pip's first dream (flavor: warm milk and moonbeams) and hired Pip on the spot as Junior Dream Engineer. First assignment: the little ghosts of Willow Lane, who hadn't slept in weeks.

🚀

Scene 3 · Every night since

Now Pip flies nightly with a lantern and a music box, delivering dreams across the rooftops. The goal: every creature asleep by moonset. Pip has never missed a night. (Pip naps a lot at sunrise.)
